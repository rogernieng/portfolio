H283ST-14A High Power Rocket

Overview

Designed and manufactured a high-power model rocket as part of an L1 rocketry certification project using the Zephyr rocket kit and an H283ST-14A motor. The project involved flight simulation, structural assembly, motor configuration, and preparation for launch.

Project Workflow

The rocket was developed through a hands-on engineering process combining flight simulation, fabrication, assembly, and testing. OpenRocket was used to evaluate the expected flight performance and verify the rocket's configuration before launch.

  • Used OpenRocket to simulate the rocket's flight trajectory, including expected altitude, velocity, stability, and motor performance.
  • Studied the construction and structural requirements of a high-power rocket, including airframe, fin, motor mount, and recovery system components.
  • Manufactured and assembled the rocket using the Zephyr rocket kit, preparing the airframe and components for flight.
  • Configured the motor delay using a Universal Delay Drill Tool (UDDT) based on the simulated flight profile.
  • Performed final preparation and launched the completed rocket as part of the L1 certification attempt.
